# About

I started writing software for a living in 2015, building web tools for an animation studio in Bengaluru, then doing product engineering at a logistics startup. In 2018 I moved to the Gulf and spent five years as a fullstack JavaScript consultant, shipping for teams at Neom, G42, Tamm and the Indian Energy Exchange, among others.

In 2023 I started [Hoomans](https://hoomans.tech). It began as a development studio doing client work, ran a staffing line for a while, and settled into what it is now: a small Dubai-based studio that designs and builds software, with one product of its own at the center.

That product is [Hadir](https://gethadir.com), a WhatsApp front desk for clinics. It started as a lead responder for any small business and narrowed to clinics first, because that's where an unanswered message costs the most. The [Now](/now/) page has the details.

Hoomans is deliberately small: me on product and strategy, a designer who handles design end to end, senior engineers handling engineering and no layers in between. I split my time between India and Dubai.
