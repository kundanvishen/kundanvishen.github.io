# Now

<p class="muted">Updated September 2026. This page tracks what I'm working on and changes often.</p>

**Hadir.** The main build. A clinic's front desk closes at 9; its patients don't. Messages that land after hours go unanswered, and the booking walks to whoever replies first. [Hadir](https://gethadir.com) answers those messages on WhatsApp from the clinic's real prices and hours, holds a slot, reminds the patient, and keeps a receipt of the revenue it recovered. Abu Dhabi first, then the rest of the Gulf. Free two-week pilots on the clinic's own number are open. If you run a clinic in the UAE and want one, email me.
